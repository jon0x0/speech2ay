## Intermittent volume investigation

`tests/measure_repeat_volume.py` records Fuse 1.7 lossless FMF audio while the
comparison's real loader and player repeat each codec three times. It reports
DC-removed whole-clip RMS, with short boundary trims. On comparison-v8, Laser
(30.wav) varied by at most 0.19 dB for AY and 0.043 dB for digitized playback;
Intruder Alert varied by at most 0.102 dB across all codecs. These runs do not
reproduce the user's intermittent quiet Laser playback and do not establish a
fix. They measure emulator PCM, not the Windows playback device or speaker.
The test bypasses keyboard selection. Use `--gap-frames 60` to add approximately
one second of idle time between repeats. Captures and reports go into a new
subdirectory of the supplied comparison build; production cartridges are not
modified.

Laser repeats with 60 extra idle frames also remained stable: maximum AY spread 0.154 dB, digitized 0.043 dB. Intermittent quiet playback remains unresolved.
