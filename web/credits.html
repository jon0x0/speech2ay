Credits and provenance

Browser emulation is provided by Josef Jelinek's TSRun. Its emulator modules, audio mixer, shaders and system ROMs load directly from the live TSRun site. This repository contains only the page adapter, not a copy of the emulator or its ROMs. See the upstream repository for their terms and provenance.

The adapter adds automatic cartridge loading, audio activation and Audio Lab controls. Initially checked against upstream revision 390ecb5d6b0dd26bf0fd1e958b25e4237369dc38; the deployed page follows the live site.

Demo recordings include Berzerk speech/effects and the Wargames “Shall we play a game” excerpt. These are demonstration material, not original recordings by this project. Their inclusion does not grant rights to reuse them. The conversion tools do not require these recordings: use your own PCM WAV files to generate speech and sound effects.
