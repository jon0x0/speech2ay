Credits and provenance

Browser emulation is provided by Josef Jelinek's TSRun. Its emulator modules, audio mixer, shaders and system ROMs load directly from the live TSRun site. The Audio Lab uses the page adapter without bundling emulator modules or ROMs. The separate AY Sound Workshop in ay-editor/ contains one unchanged local copy of TSRun's ay.js as ay-core.mjs, inherited from the Sinistar editor; its hash is recorded in ay-editor/provenance.json. It needs no system ROM. See the upstream repository for the core's terms and provenance; this project grants no new license for that dependency. Workshop includes the accepted Sinistar 30-frame gunshot AY fit, adapted from RemingtonGunshot.wav by fastson under CC BY 3.0. The recording was cropped and level-adjusted before noise/volume fitting; no source PCM is bundled. The other starter sounds are synthetic parameter examples.

The adapter adds automatic cartridge loading, audio activation and Audio Lab controls. Initially checked against upstream revision 390ecb5d6b0dd26bf0fd1e958b25e4237369dc38; the deployed page follows the live site.

Demo recordings include Berzerk speech/effects and the Wargames “Shall we play a game” excerpt. These are demonstration material, not original recordings by this project. Their inclusion does not grant rights to reuse them. The conversion tools do not require these recordings: use your own PCM WAV files to generate speech and sound effects.
